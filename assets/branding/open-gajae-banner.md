# Open-gajae banner

Mode: built-in image_gen. Reference: earlier banner artwork (removed during cleanup). Attribution and license are preserved below. Design changes do not establish legal clearance.

## Prompt

Edit this 2048x768 pixel-art banner with exactly these changes:
1. Small coral lobster claw badge at lower-left: REMOVE ALL teeth, bumps, serrations from the INSIDE of both jaws. Leave simple clean concave inner edges with no teeth. Preserve current anatomy, continuous long fixed jaw and small opposing jaw, plump body, size, pose, placement and coral/salmon palette. Restore unmistakable COARSE PIXEL ART: visible square stair-step edges and chunky square highlight/shadow clusters, same pixel grid scale as banner title. No smooth anti-aliased curves or tiny pixels. Curves are built from deliberate square steps. Keep rich shell facets.
2. Replace the rigid rectangular ivory ring icon with a friendly rounded pixel capital O. Keep approximate original width/height, center, ivory palette and restrained dimensional shading. ROUND ALL FOUR outer corners significantly with 3–4 clear square stair steps each, making an elongated oval O rather than a rectangular doorframe. Round the INNER hole corners with matching pixel stair steps as well. Continuous consistent thick stroke. Flat short top and bottom with stepped rounded shoulders, gently curved sides expressed in square blocks. It must read as a rounded retro videogame letter O. No diagonal smooth bevel cuts. Preserve dark recessed interior and dark gray backing tile.
Keep everything else unchanged: gray stepped tile placement, dark solid charcoal background, exact text "open-gajae" and "Clarify. Plan. Execute.", typography, layout and spacing. No extra text or decorations. Matte crisp pixel art. No glow, bloom, halos or light effects.

## Pixel refinement

Only change rendering of CORAL CLAW BADGE, leave all other artwork exactly unchanged including new rounded pixel O, tile, typography and background.
Re-render the SAME claw silhouette and shading on a visibly coarse square pixel grid, approximately 8x8 screen-pixel blocks at this 2048x768 resolution. Like a 28x32 pixel game sprite enlarged with NEAREST NEIGHBOR. All outer and inner edges have obvious horizontal/vertical stair steps. Highlights and shadows are contiguous chunky square pixel clusters, NOT smooth digital painting. Keep anatomical curves represented by square steps, same plumpness, two jaws, same size/position/coral colors. NO TEETH or bumps inside jaws; clean toothless opening. Preserve continuous upper jaw connection to palm. Do not add seams. No glow or bloom. Pixelate only claw, preserving icon and text.

## Matte cleanup

Remove all bloom, glow, white and red halos from this image. Flat uniform charcoal #191A1C background. Keep all geometry, coarse pixel claw, toothless jaws, rounded pixel O, gray tile and exact typography unchanged. Only remove atmospheric light outside objects. Sharp matte pixel artwork.
